Undergraduate Thesis

Agricultural Research

Application of Microbead Biofertilizer, Bioameliorant Briquette, and Phosphorus Fertilizer to Improve Soil Biology-Chemistry, Growth, and Yield of Maize on Inceptisols

Research Objective

Analyze the interaction between microbead biofertilizer, bioameliorant briquette, and phosphorus fertilizer on soil properties, phosphate-solubilizing microorganisms, plant growth, and maize productivity.

Experimental Design

Factorial Randomized Block Design (RAK Faktorial) 4 × 5 treatment combinations with 3 replications.

Materials Used

Microbead alginate carrier, bioameliorant briquette, rock phosphate (RP), and SP-36 fertilizer.

Research Impact

Evaluated sustainable phosphorus management strategies through biofertilizer application and reduced dependence on conventional phosphorus fertilizer.

Key Findings

  • Microbead and bioameliorant application improved soil biological and chemical characteristics.
  • Combination treatment with reduced rock phosphate dose showed potential comparable effectiveness with conventional phosphorus fertilizer.
  • Research supports sustainable agriculture through improved nutrient efficiency and soil health.
